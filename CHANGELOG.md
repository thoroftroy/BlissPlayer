# Bliss Player changelog

## v0.14.73

- The About section now links to the rest of the Wonderdesk suite: check for updates in App Hub, send feedback with Pulse, and set the look in Control Room

## v0.14.70

- Music now carries on after you use voice typing: Bliss keeps watching after a pause caused by dictation and resumes once the microphone is free (or after a few quiet seconds if your keyboard hides the microphone), and leaves it alone if another app is really playing audio

## v0.14.66

- The playback controls (previous, play or pause, next, favourite) also appear on the Wonderdesk Launcher's floating button, when it is in use. The notification is unchanged. Turn it off in Control Room


## v0.14.63

- Crashes are now reported the same way as every other Wonderdesk app, through Control Room, instead of only being kept in this app

## v0.14.60

- Music now carries on by itself after voice typing, however long you dictate. Before, a long dictation left it paused until you reopened the app

## v0.14.59

- Text size chosen in Control Room now applies here too, so the player matches the rest of the Wonderdesk suite
- The suite accent colour from Control Room is read as well, wherever the player uses its accent
- Nothing changes if Control Room is not installed
